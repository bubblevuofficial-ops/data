Financial assets are classified into three subsequent measurement methods:

1. *FVTPL (Fair Value through Profit or Loss):*
- For assets not meeting amortised cost or FVTOCI criteria.
- Also chosen by the entity.
- Valued at fair value, changes affect profit/loss.

2. *FVTOCI (Fair Value through Other Comprehensive Income):*
- For assets held for cash flows and profitability.
- Valued at fair value, changes affect other comprehensive income (OCI).

3. *Amortised Cost:*
- For assets held for cash flows only.
- Valued at cost, with interest recognized over time.
